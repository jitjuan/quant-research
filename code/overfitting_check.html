Combinatorially Symmetric Cross-Validation (Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado & Zhu, 2014) over the same 9 configurations: split the sample into 16 blocks, form every 8-vs-8 train/test split, pick the in-sample winner each time, and see how often it lands below the out-of-sample median.
