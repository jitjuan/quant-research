Time-Series Momentum & MA Crossover on a Simulated Equity Index

Trailing-return momentum at three lookbacks, plus a 20/100-day moving-average crossover, all executed next day with a 1 bp cost per unit of position traded. No real-time market data feed is connected in this environment, so prices below are a calibrated simulation, not live data — methodology holds regardless of the data source.

Momentum signal. At the close of day t, compute the trailing N-day return (N = 63, 126, 252). Go long if positive, short if negative.
Crossover signal. At the close of day t, compare the 20-day and 100-day moving averages. Long when fast > slow, short when fast < slow.
Execution lag. Every signal above is only acted on the next trading day — no look-ahead, enforced the same way for all four rules.
Cost & universe. 1bp per unit of position changed, single simulated index, fully invested long or short (±100%), no leverage.
